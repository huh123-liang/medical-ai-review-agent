# Third-party notices for kb/v3

`healthbench-items.jsonl` contains the consensus-criterion texts of the HealthBench meta-evaluation set
(Arora et al., 2025, arXiv:2505.08775), distributed in openai/simple-evals under the MIT License reproduced below.
One criterion (`HB:hedging:no-uncertainty:hedges`) was modified by this project: the polarity of step 2 was
corrected (see the item's `source.note`). `situations-healthbench.jsonl` names follow the HealthBench taxonomy;
the `applies_when` texts and the dimension mapping are this project's own work.

The disease reference notes paraphrase public guidance (US FDA Drug Safety Communication 2016-04-08; Merck
Manual Professional Version) and are cited in each item's `source`.
